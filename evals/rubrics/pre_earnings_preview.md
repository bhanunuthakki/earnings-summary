# Rubric: pre_earnings_preview (v2)

Pass threshold: 0.80
Scope: Pre-earnings setup and preview briefs framing expectations, what's priced in ahead of the print, load-bearing call questions, and post-print decision triggers.
Grading convention: 1.0 = clear pass, 0.5 = partial, 0.0 = fail.

Include: _shared_mbi_definitions.md

## Facet: whats_priced_in_ahead_of_print [weight: 0.25] — Connects near-term bar to multi-year market expectations
- **1.0:** Establishes the exact operational baseline in a compact multi-quarter trend-to-bar table (`Q-4..Q0 -> Q+1E`) alongside guidance and dated consensus (or concise gap disclosure), and explains what the current stock price assumes both for this quarter and for the multi-year trajectory — separating one-quarter timing noise from real expectation shifts.
- **0.5:** Recites prior results and guidance accurately in prose without a multi-quarter trend view or connecting them to what is already priced into the stock.
- **0.0:** Missing or inaccurate baseline, or fails to frame what is priced in heading into the print.

## Facet: load_bearing_operational_questions [weight: 0.25] — Questions dig into cohorts, underwriting/delivery, and call nuance
- **1.0:** Focuses questions on the 2–3 operational mechanisms that actually move the multi-year thesis: cohort monetization and retention mechanics, credit/loss or commercial-meter reality, capital/funding tradeoffs, and competitive profit-pool dynamics — not generic headline beat/miss trivia.
- **0.5:** Asks surface-level KPI questions ("did NIM expand?", "did ARR beat?") without digging into underlying operational causality.
- **0.0:** Generic sector boilerplate questions.

## Facet: asymmetry_candor_and_imagination [weight: 0.20] — Low-confidence assumptions, upside imagination, and downside risk
- **1.0:** Candidly names what remains low-confidence ahead of the print, maps asymmetric paths tied to multi-year compounding IRR rather than guessing the 1-day stock move (adding an optional illustrative unit-math or scenario table when the preview involves a major model/DCF revision or structural unit-economics debate, or concise bounded path math otherwise), and considers both downside breaking points and where the market might be under-imagining multi-year operational compounding.
- **0.5:** Lists upside/downside cases vaguely without bounded path math or tying them to specific low-confidence assumptions and long-term compounding asymmetry.
- **0.0:** Predicts short-term price action or omits downside asymmetry.

## Facet: say_do_and_disclosure_traps [weight: 0.20] — Tracks prior promises, wording drift, and metric scope
- **1.0:** Audits prior guidance credibility, subtle management wording drift, non-GAAP exclusions, one-offs, and smoothed or unmatched comparison charts to watch for on the upcoming call.
- **0.5:** Mentions prior guidance briefly without spotlighting wording drift, scope mismatches, or missing disclosures.
- **0.0:** Accepts management framing at face value with no say/do check.

## Facet: decision_clarity_and_mbi_voice [weight: 0.10] — Plain-spoken solo-investor judgment and clear post-print triggers
- **1.0:** Clear, engaging MBI prose free of sell-side hype, anticipatory trade directives (`Buy/Sell before earnings`), and leaked internal pipeline/file/JSON identifiers (such as `"the packet"` or raw schema field names), closing with crisp "if print shows X, re-underwrite Y" triggers tied to the empirical thesis.
- **0.5:** Lists metrics to watch without specifying what evidence would actually alter conviction, slips into stiff template prose, or leaks internal pipeline/schema jargon.
- **0.0:** Pre-earnings trading call or template boilerplate.
