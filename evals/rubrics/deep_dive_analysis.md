# Rubric: deep_dive_analysis (v1)

Pass threshold: 0.70

Scope: one long-form company research memo, brief, or deep-dive evaluation
from `output/research/`. Graded against the analytical discipline observed in
Mostly Borrowed Ideas (MBI Deep Dives) company analyses and models: unit-driver
decomposition, footnote-to-model linkage, priced-in reverse DCF, substantive
counter-checks, and factual provenance.

Grading convention per facet: 1.0 = met cleanly across the report; 0.5 =
partially met or uneven across sections; 0.0 = broadly missed. Judge ONLY the
supplied report text; do not invent outside facts or reward length.

## Facet: driver_and_unit_discipline — decomposes economics into physical/unit drivers with consistent denominators

Revenue and cost narratives must decompose headline numbers into concrete
operating drivers (e.g., volume/users × price/frequency/ARPU, take rates,
compute GW or capacity, square footage, procedure mix) rather than treating
top-line growth percentages as self-explanatory. Denominators must remain
economically consistent across comparisons (e.g., costs over GMV vs. GAAP
revenue, FCF after SBC, capex separated from D&A).

## Facet: footnote_to_model_linkage — connects filing footnotes and non-P&L mechanics to forecast assumptions

Analysis must surface and link non-obvious filing, footnote, or proxy mechanics
— such as non-cancelable purchase commitments, construction-in-progress and
depreciation useful-life shifts, SBC/dilution overhang, proxy equity hurdles,
RPO/backlog, or segment/accounting reclassifications — directly into operating
or valuation assumptions, rather than stopping at headline P&L lines.

## Facet: priced_in_reverse_dcf — frames what current price implies with IRR/multiple consistency and sensitivities

Valuation must reason explicitly about what operating assumptions the current
share price implies (reverse DCF, multi-year IRR, or explicit target-multiple
math grounded in mature ROIC/reinvestment), rather than asserting a stock is
"cheap" or "expensive" from a single trailing multiple. Key assumptions must
carry concrete sensitivities or scenario bounds showing what flips the view.

## Facet: steelman_and_cross_check — tests thesis with peer/value-chain checks, counter-cases, and explicit uncertainty

Must engage a substantive, company-specific disconfirming case and cross-check
unit economics or capital intensity against named peers, competitors, or value-
chain participants on comparable bases. Must explicitly mark speculative
assumptions, data limits, and unresolved debates instead of projecting false
precision.

## Facet: grounding_and_provenance — separates reported facts, management claims, estimates, and inference with citations

Every material figure must carry period, unit/currency, and source attribution
(filing, transcript, or labeled estimate), keeping reported facts, management
claims, consensus estimates, calculations, and analyst inference clearly
distinguishable with zero fabricated numbers or unsupported comparisons.
