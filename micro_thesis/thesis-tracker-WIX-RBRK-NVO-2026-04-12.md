### Adversarial stress test

**What happened:**
Three compounding forces collapsed the thesis simultaneously:

1. **MFN pricing:** US government Most-Favored-Nation pricing agreement is compressing Wegovy/Ozempic US pricing. Search results indicate generic rivals forcing 48% price cuts in select markets.
2. **Semaglutide LOE:** Patent expiry in China, India, Brazil, Canada in 2026 opens the door to local generic competition in markets representing material international revenue.
3. **Competition:** Eli Lilly's tirzepatide (Mounjaro/Zepbound) has taken share in both diabetes and obesity. Ozempic Q4 CER growth of +1% confirms market share pressure.

**Bull counter-argument:**
- CagriSema NDA filed with FDA. Redefine 1 trial: 91.9% of patients achieved ≥5% weight loss vs. 31.5% placebo. If approved (expected review 2026), CagriSema could reignite the obesity franchise with a differentiated next-gen product.
- Oral semaglutide for obesity remains a potential TAM expansion.
- The 2026 decline may be a one-time pricing reset, not structural demand destruction. Obesity is still a secular growth category.

**Post-dialectic assessment:**
The bull case is real but it's a **"wait for the next catalyst"** thesis, not a **"the original thesis holds"** thesis. The original thesis — durable 20%+ CER growth from GLP-1 scale-up — is broken by the data. Ozempic has flatlined. Wegovy is decelerating. Pricing power is eroding. CagriSema is 12-18 months from meaningful revenue even under optimistic approval timelines.

The question is whether to hold for the CagriSema optionality or exit the position. That is a Hold/Sell matrix question, not a thesis-tracker question.

**Action:** **Hold/Sell matrix review required.** Key decision inputs: (1) CagriSema FDA timeline and competitive landscape vs. Lilly retatrutide; (2) magnitude of MFN pricing compression on 2026 actuals vs. guide; (3) whether stock at -43% YoY already prices in the thesis break.
