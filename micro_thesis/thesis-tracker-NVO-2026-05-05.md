The REDEFINE-1 primary endpoint is cleared but the REDEFINE-4 head-to-head result — arguably more commercially determinative — remains outstanding in the staleness gap.

- **DKK 17B in business development deployed in 2025**: Akero acquisition (efruxifermin, Phase 3 MASH in compensated cirrhosis F4), Metsera acquisition (USD 7.2B + up to USD 2.8B CVRs, oral peptide/peptide engineering capabilities), and Zaltenibart (MASP-3 inhibitor, rare blood/kidney disorders) [Source: Q3 Presentation Brief, Q3 2025; Q4 Press Release, FY 2025]; 9,000-headcount restructuring with ~DKK 8B annualized savings [Source: Q3 Presentation Brief, Q3 2025] alongside DKK 9B one-off charge [Source: Q3 Presentation Brief, Q3 2025].
