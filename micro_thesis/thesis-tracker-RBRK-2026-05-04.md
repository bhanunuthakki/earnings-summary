### Subscription Revenue Growth — distance to break: well above 25% currently; FY27 guide implies near-threshold
- **Primary Thesis:** FY27 total revenue guide of $1,597–$1,607M [Source: presentation, Q4 2026, slide 27] vs. FY26 $1,316.2M [Source: 10-K, FY 2026, MD&A revenue table] = ~21.5% reported growth, normalized to ~27–28% per management once the $60M material-rights step-down is removed [Source: presentation, Q4 2026, slide 28]. The break condition is "Subscription revenue growth < 25% YoY for 2 consecutive quarters" — currently a wide margin on normalized basis.
- **Strongest Counter:** Reported (not normalized) Q4 subscription revenue growth in FY27 will compress sharply as material-rights revenue collapses from $25M (Q3 FY26) and $18M (Q4 FY26) to a mere ~$4M in Q1 FY27 [Source: presentation, Q4 2026, slide 28 & slide 29]. Schema does not specify whether the 25% threshold is reported or normalized — that ambiguity is itself a thesis-tracker gap.
- **Resolution:** On a normalized basis the trigger is comfortably clear; on a reported basis, the threshold could be tested. Net Conviction: **Medium**. Confirm-or-clear observable: Q1 FY27 reported subscription revenue YoY — and explicit clarification in the schema on whether the 25% threshold is reported or excludes material rights.
- **Sensitivity:** A FY27 normalized growth print of 22% (5pts below management's own framing) would put two consecutive sub-25% quarters on the table.
