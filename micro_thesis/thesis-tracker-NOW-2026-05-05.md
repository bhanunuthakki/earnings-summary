## Thesis Status: 🟢 INTACT

All three Tier-1 KPIs are at or above their thresholds, with the key driver — Now Assist >$1M ACV customer growth at >130% YoY [Source: Press Release, Q1 2026, Key Business Metrics] — tracking 80pp above its <50% break condition.

### Adversarial Loop — Thesis Status (REQUIRED)

- **Primary Thesis:** The primary AI monetization signal is unambiguously above break: Now Assist >$1M ACV customer cohort grew >130% YoY in Q1 2026 [Source: Press Release, Q1 2026, Key Business Metrics], and the confirming Tier-2 signal ($5M+ ACV at 630 customers, +22% YoY [Source: Press Release, Q1 2026, Key Business Metrics]) indicates enterprise up-tiering is not isolated to a single cohort. cRPO cc at 21% [Source: Press Release, Q1 2026, cRPO] provides durable forward-revenue visibility well above the 18% break.

- **Strongest Counter:** The >130% growth rate is computed on an absolute base that is nowhere disclosed [Source: Pass A, Schema Hygiene — "absolute customer count [not disclosed]"]. The >$5M ACV cohort — a more mature population — is growing at only 22% YoY [Source: Press Release, Q1 2026, Key Business Metrics], which is the plausible endpoint for the >$1M cohort as it seasons. If the >$1M ACV cohort today is, say, 200–300 customers, doubling to 400–600 in two years is achievable; if it is already 800+, the law of large numbers compresses the growth rate quickly. The thesis asserts a step-function ARPU uplift from Now Assist, but Pro+ SKU-level revenue contribution remains entirely opaque [Source: Pass A, Schema Hygiene, KPI 3 note], so the flow-through from cohort growth to subscription revenue cc is an inferred residual, not a disclosed link.

- **Resolution:** The key driver is tracking far above break and neither the Q4 2025 nor Q1 2026 corpus contains a single directly contradicting data point. However, the undisclosed base makes the >130% figure un-stress-testable, and the mature-cohort ($5M+ ACV at +22%) provides a sobering ceiling for what the younger cohort's trajectory looks like at scale. **Net Conviction: Medium.** Flip-the-verdict observable: Now Assist >$1M ACV YoY growth decelerating to ≤80% in Q2 2026 (the midpoint between current 130% and 50% break), which would signal compounding is faltering as the cohort broadens beyond early adopters — consistent with the adversarial take in the schema.

- **Sensitivity:** If Now Assist >$1M ACV growth decelerates to 80% YoY, it remains above the 50% break condition for at minimum two more quarters even at linear deceleration of 25pp/quarter. The thesis does not break on a single print; the compounding-collapse scenario requires at least two consecutive quarters of sharp deceleration. Separately, if Now Assist monetization flows through at 30–40% incremental gross margin (vs. the 82% subscription gross margin guided for FY2026 [Source: Press Release, Q1 2026, Guidance — FY2026 gross margin]), the AI ARPU uplift thesis could hold on cohort growth metrics while failing to accelerate top-line cc materially above organic baseline.

If Q2 cc < 18.5%, the break condition is within one quarter's deceleration of being live, and the two-consecutive-quarter framework for thesis review should activate.

- **Sensitivity:** A compounding Middle East headwind of 100bps per quarter (vs. disclosed 75bps in Q1) plus 50bps of Armis revenue recognition delay would compress Q2 cc sub rev to approximately 18–18.5% — inside the 17% break by only 50–150bps.
