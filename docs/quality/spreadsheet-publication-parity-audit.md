## First removable competing procedure and blockers

**No persisted writer procedure is proven removable yet.** The precise future cut is this producer's route through `persist_manifest` → `insert_kpi_with_restatement_detection` → legacy `resolve_fact_row`, once an equivalent native publication exists. This does not authorize deleting shared owners or legacy history; they have other callers.

The bounded blockers are: (1) exact cell locator/raw value and extraction population are lost at `parse_spreadsheet` output; (2) the fixture has no explicit native reporting identity, period start/kind, scope/basis or definition binding; (3) legacy unknown/rejected/quarantined dispositions lack same-input native parity evidence; (4) candidate/supersession and point-in-time outputs are not compared; (5) attempt replay, partial-period commits and restart behavior differ. The native repository already owns graph publication, replay and rollback. No new store or framework is needed to assess these gaps. This audit does not establish runtime non-use or permission to remove any alternate table.
