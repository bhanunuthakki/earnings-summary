# Public-evidence research method

Use for company briefs/reports, pre/post-earnings work and portfolio or evaluation
theses. This method owns analytical mechanics inside existing artifact contracts.
[Routes](routes.md) own execution; [analysis paths](analysis-paths.md) own modes.
It adds no artifact schema, accepted threshold, acquisition route or write permission.

## Evidence to judgment

1. Fix the issuer/security, fiscal period, review date, knowledge cutoff, artifact
   and portfolio/evaluation status. Recover the accepted thesis or label the
   evaluation hypothesis as draft. Identify the current model and dated prior
   review. Do not treat a missing prior review as evidence of no change.
2. Establish the evidence package through existing readers. Record document
   identity, date, fiscal basis and page/table/segment locators. Keep acquisition,
   extraction and semantic admission separate. For transcripts, record prepared
   remarks and question-and-answer (Q&A) coverage, included/omitted ranges and
   whether the complete relevant exchange is available. Unknown coverage stays
   unknown. Preserve source/context manifests or claim-level citations.
3. Select the questions that could change a thesis pillar, forecast assumption,
   risk assessment or research decision. Test each against supporting evidence,
   contrary evidence and the strongest plausible alternative cause. Distinguish
   reported fact, management claim, expectation, calculation and analyst inference.
   A sourced quote proves what management said; it does not prove the claim.
4. Form a working record for each material finding: prior view/bar and date;
   new evidence and locator; supporting and contrary readings; affected thesis
   pillar/model assumption; conclusion and evidence limits; next public check.
   Use existing artifact fields or cited prose, not a new mandatory database.
5. Check arithmetic with existing calculation owners. Verify comparable periods,
   definitions, scope, units and accounting basis. Place findings in the existing
   sections below. Keep only decision-relevant detail in the synthesis and retain
   its underlying evidence. An unresolved material question is a valid finding.

## Moat: test the economic mechanism

For each material claimed advantage, connect: **advantage → economic mechanism →
public evidence/counter-evidence → alternative explanation → judgment/next check**.
Examples of mechanisms include lower service cost, switching costs, network
effects, distribution advantage and pricing power. State the exposed competitor
or substitute when evidence supports one. Do not invent competitive statistics.

Growth, market share and margin are outcomes. They can support a mechanism but
do not establish its cause or durability. Test mix, price, geography, acquisitions,
cycle, subsidies, risk-taking or accounting changes where relevant. Preserve
differences across products/segments. A strong business can still be expensive.

Every proposed check must name a public source, expected disclosure/event,
observable condition and investment consequence. Use issuer filings, earnings
releases, presentations and transcripts first. A disclosed proxy must carry its
limitation. Do not assign private cohort, churn, experiment or underwriting-model
tests as retail-investor homework. Optional third-party evidence cannot silently
replace an accepted issuer metric. If the mechanism cannot be tested publicly,
say **unproven from public evidence** and explain what remains uncertain. Missing
disclosure does not itself prove moat deterioration or change an approved rule.

## Earnings: preserve the bar before judging the result

Before earnings, record each priority's observable, dated baseline/source,
confirming and adverse outcome, and thesis consequence. Separate management
guidance, sourced consensus and owner/analyst expectations. An analyst question
or model assumption is not consensus. Numeric bars require comparable evidence;
otherwise use a specific qualitative question without an invented threshold.

After earnings, compare actuals with that same dated bar and the accepted rules.
Distinguish the current result, revised guidance and a changed forecast. Check
definition or basis changes before calculating a beat/miss. If a saved pre-call
baseline is absent, say so. A dated prior guidance comparison can still be made;
do not reconstruct the owner's expectations after seeing results.

For historical work, distinguish evidence known at the call from subsequent
information and the current investment view. If the runtime cannot enforce the
requested cutoff, label the mixed context and restrict the conclusion. Do not
present it as a reconstruction of what an investor knew then.

## Q&A: assess the complete exchange

For each material question, recover the question and every management response
and follow-up relevant to it. Split multipart questions into requested components.
Record analyst/speaker, topic, asked-for information, response segment locators,
information supplied and unresolved components. Classify each component as:

- **Answered:** the requested information is supplied, including a later response.
- **Partly answered:** relevant information is supplied but a component remains open.
- **Explicitly declined:** management states it will not provide the information.
- **Not addressed:** the complete exchange is available and the component receives
  no substantive response.
- **Insufficient transcript evidence:** missing, truncated or ambiguous material
  prevents a reliable classification.

Report the material unresolved component and its investment consequence. Use
neutral wording such as “did not quantify credit costs by vintage.” Do not infer
deception, motive or poor business results from a non-answer. An explicit refusal
is different from an overlooked component. Missing Q&A cannot prove avoidance.
Do not convert a question-topic tag into an answer-completeness finding.

## Language and management credibility

Compare cited passages on the same topic, speaker/role and prepared-versus-Q&A
context. Record the comparison dates, wording, qualifications and specificity of
commitments. Account for changed question framing, personnel, disclosure policy
and business conditions. A new speaker prevents a personal before/after claim;
describe a management-level wording change only with that limitation.

Describe confidence, hedging, specificity or reversals as textual observations
and analyst interpretation. Text does not establish vocal tone or emotion. Do
not assign calibrated probabilities or a generic sentiment score without a
qualified method. A “dropped topic” claim requires adequate comparable coverage.
Report no material language change when that is the supported result.

For say-do, compare dated commitments with comparable outcomes. Separate missed
commitments from unresolved outcomes and changed definitions. Test management
execution against external causes. Treat self-reported success as a claim until
supported. Material contradictory wording can raise a question; it cannot alone
establish management dishonesty or a thesis break.

## Placement within existing artifacts

Preserve the route's section inventory and business-model relevance rules. The
method changes the analysis inside sections; it does not require new sections.

| Existing company brief/report content | Analytical responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Portfolio/evaluation snapshot and portfolio position | Status, dated thesis/hypothesis and decision context; suitability/sizing needs the full-book allocation path |
| Company description; financials; segments; peer/comparison context | Business drivers, mechanism-based moat evidence, comparability and alternative causes; no forced number/competitor in every paragraph |
| Thesis and bear case | Accepted pillar versus draft hypothesis; strongest company-specific counter; supported public proof/disproof; avoid “non-consensus” claims without a consensus reference |
| Earnings; say-do; Q&A roster; transcript appendix | Dated bar/result, complete exchanges, matched language and commitments; roster/appendix remain evidence access rather than inferred judgment |
| Valuation | Exact affected model assumption and existing calculation/scenario; if assumptions are absent, name what requires review without inventing fair value |
| Signals; recent developments; filing intelligence; executive compensation | Material supporting/contrary evidence, time relevance and incentive implications; a signal is not proof of cause |
| IR documents; provenance; supplementary data | Source access, coverage, missing/stale inputs and lineage; context panels stay available without forced investment claims |
| Synthesis and Investment Decision Card | What changed, why it matters, strongest counter, confidence limits and next public proof; distinguish company quality, security value and portfolio action |

Pre-earnings retains its four sections:

| Section | Required analysis |
| --- | --- |
| What this quarter must show | Prioritize the thesis outcomes and record the dated bar |
| Numbers to check the moment they print | Public observables, comparable baselines and approved threshold implications |
| What to listen for on the call | Specific material questions, earlier unresolved components and comparable language/commitments |
| Thesis pressure points | Confirming/adverse outcomes, affected assumptions and supported decision after each outcome |

Post-earnings retains its five sections:

| Section | Required analysis |
| --- | --- |
| Quarter in one line | Most material result and its investment significance, with evidence limits |
| What changed versus expectations | Same dated pre-call bar; guidance/consensus/owner distinctions; baseline gaps |
| What management said | Material explanations, complete-exchange answer status and matched language changes |
| Thesis update | Accepted rules plus material moat/counter evidence; what strengthened, weakened, stayed unchanged or remains unresolved |
| What to verify next quarter | Public source, disclosure/event, observable and thesis/model consequence for each material check |

For portfolio names, preserve accepted thresholds and disclose unresolved rule
inputs. Qualitative research can request re-underwriting; it cannot override a
breach. For evaluation names, state preliminary coverage and the next proof
needed to develop the hypothesis. Do not grant readiness, change evaluation
level or recommend a position size from company analysis alone.

## Delivery and runtime limits

Check that each material conclusion has a source/locator, an explicit inference
when needed, a substantive counter and a public next check. Avoid filler, forced
precision and repeated passages across sections. If an analysis does not change
the view, say that briefly. A simple factual answer does not need every test.

The application adapter in `src/research/method_contract.py` loads this source
for the affected brief, readout, company, thesis, bear, language and five-minute
prompts. The pre/post generators bind the rendered prompt and method identity,
check their section inventory, and reject oversize complete inputs. Inspect the
actual route and installed revision before claiming enforcement. Missing prior
briefs, absent model assumptions or unsupported comparisons can prevent analysis. Deliver a bounded cited assessment or unresolved finding
and identify the owning capability gap. Do not mark a cached artifact as improved
because this file changed, or bypass schema/provenance gates with a prose overlay.
Evaluate instruction behavior with [research cases](research-cases.json) through
the existing [assessment loop](assessment.md); application prompt/model changes
retain their own evaluation and promotion gates.
