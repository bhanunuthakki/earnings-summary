# Uber Technologies (UBER) — Bull / Bear / Valuation Deep-Dive
*Prepared 2026-06-07 · figures verified against Q1 2026 results (qtr ended 3/31/26, reported 5/6/26) and live quotes*

## 1. Snapshot
- **Price:** ~$70.7 (6/5/26 close; down from ~$75 on 5/18). **Market cap ~$144B.** 52-wk range $68–$102 — the stock sits near its **52-week low** despite record fundamentals.
- **Segments:** Mobility (ride-hailing, ~$26.4B Q1 GB), Delivery (food/grocery/retail, ~$26.0B GB), Freight (~$1.3B, low-margin logistics). Mobility and Delivery are now roughly co-equal in bookings.
- **Central debate:** A now durably profitable, FCF-gushing two-sided network compounding bookings ~20–25% with a high-margin advertising/membership layer at a sub-1 PEG — **versus** the existential question of whether autonomous vehicles (Waymo, Tesla, Zoox) disintermediate the driver marketplace that is Uber's moat.

## 2. Bull case (what must go right)
1. **Bookings re-accelerated, not decelerated.** Q1 GB +25% YoY to **$53.7B** (+21% cc); Mobility GB +20%, Delivery +23%, Freight back to growth. Trips +20% to **3.6B**, on MAPCs +17% and frequency +3%. This is acceleration off a $50B+ quarterly base — rare at this scale.
2. **Margin expansion is structural.** Adj EBITDA +33% to **$2.5B** (4.6% of GB vs ~4.0% prior); GAAP operating income +57% to **$1.9B** (14.6% margin vs 10.6%). The platform shows operating leverage as fixed costs amortize over more trips.
3. **The FCF inflection is the real story.** Q1 FCF **$2.3B** (~92% of adj EBITDA); **FY2025 FCF ~$10B**. This is an asset-light model converting EBITDA to cash at >90% — funding a **$20B buyback authorization** (announced 8/25), with a **record $3B repurchased in Q1 alone** (~2% of cap in one quarter).
4. **Advertising is a high-margin layer compounding fast.** Now **>$2B annualized run-rate, +50% YoY**, at near-software incremental margins — accretive to take rate and largely independent of AV outcomes (sponsored listings monetize the demand graph, not the driver).
5. **Uber One flywheel:** **50M members (+50% YoY), >50% of gross bookings.** Membership lowers churn, lifts frequency, and bundles Mobility+Delivery — a structural defense and the cross-sell engine.

## 3. Bear case (genuine skeptic)
1. **AV/robotaxi — the thesis-defining risk.** *Bear:* If driverless economics win, Uber's value-add (matching riders to a human driver) compresses; a vertically integrated fleet owner (Tesla, Waymo) could route demand through its own app and capture the driver's ~75% margin share. Tesla is scaling Austin to 245 sq mi; Waymo is shifting **from Uber partner toward direct competitor** in SF/Dallas/London. *Nuance:* Uber is hedging aggressively — **$300M+ into Lucid, $300M+ into Nuro (20,000+ vehicles), an Nvidia alliance targeting 100,000 AVs by 2027, robotaxis in 15+ cities in 2026.** The bull framing: AV fleets need demand aggregation, fleet utilization, and last-mile logistics that Uber already owns. **Which way this breaks is the single biggest swing factor** and is genuinely unresolved.
2. **GAAP earnings are noisy and the headline P/E lies.** Q1 GAAP net income was just **$263M / $0.13 EPS** — depressed by a **$1.5B pre-tax equity-revaluation headwind** (Didi stake fell $3.0B→$2.3B; Aurora moved $1.25B→$1.34B). Prior-year net was *flattered* by the same mechanism plus tax items. Reported P/E (~17–25x depending on source) is unreliable in both directions.
3. **Gig-worker reclassification** remains a live tail risk (EU Platform Work Directive, US state actions). Reclassifying drivers as employees would inflate the cost base and compress Mobility margins materially.
4. **Take-rate and competitive ceilings.** Blended take is already ~24.6% (rev/GB; Mobility 30%+). Pushing higher invites driver/regulator pushback; DoorDash (Delivery) and regional players cap pricing power.
5. **Insurance-cost inflation** has been a persistent Mobility margin drag, and **valuation has no cushion if growth slows** — at ~20x EV/EBITDA, decelerating to ~10–12% bookings growth would compress the multiple hard.

## 4. Valuation
**Anchor on FCF and EV/EBITDA, not GAAP P/E** — reported EPS is distorted by mark-to-market equity stakes (a $1.5B swing this quarter) and one-time tax items. On cash, ~$144B cap / ~$10B FY25 FCF ≈ **14–15x P/FCF**, and **EV/EBITDA ~20x** (trailing) compressing toward the mid-teens on forward estimates.

- **Reverse-DCF / what's priced in:** At ~$71 the market is implicitly pricing high-single-digit growth and *no* AV upside — arguably already discounting an AV-disruption scenario, given bookings are compounding 20–25% and FCF is ~$10B. The gap between the price (near 52-wk low) and fundamentals (record quarter) is the setup.
- **Scenario targets (12–24mo):**

| Scenario | Bookings CAGR | Adj EBITDA margin (% GB) | FY27E EBITDA | EV/EBITDA | Implied price | Return |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | ~22% | ~5.5% | ~$15B | 22x | **~$135** | **+90%** |
| **Base** | ~16% | ~5.0% | ~$13B | 16x | **~$100** | **+40%** |
| **Bear** (AV/reg) | ~8% | ~4.2% | ~$10.5B | 10x | **~$52** | **−27%** |

Base case (~$100) brackets the **consensus ~$104–108** target. The asymmetry is favorable but **fat-tailed on the downside** because of AV.

## 5. Portfolio fit
- **Diversification:** Strong. The book is concentrated in LatAm (MELI+NU ≈24%), enterprise software (NOW/VEEV/RBRK/WIX ≈28%), and ~24% US index. UBER is a US-centric consumer-platform/transport name — a **new factor exposure**. The screen's marginal-Sharpe **+0.44** and correlation **0.48** confirm it diversifies despite high standalone vol (38%).
- **MELI overlap (watch this):** Real but partial. MELI runs Mercado Envíos (logistics/last-mile) + Mercado Pago (fintech) + ad network — thematically rhyming with Uber's delivery + payments + ads. Both are **"super-app / logistics + ads + payments" compounders**, but geographies don't overlap (LatAm vs US/global) and Uber has no fintech-lending balance-sheet risk. Net: thematic, not redundant — but it does *add* to an already-large "consumer logistics + embedded ads" tilt.
- **Sizing:** Given the favorable marginal-Sharpe but the binary AV tail and 38% vol, **initiate at ~3–4%** (between SGOV-tier and the software single-names), with room to add toward 5–6% on AV-economics confirmation or a sub-$65 entry. Size it as a *call-option-on-platform-durability*, not a core holding.

## 6. Key diligence questions / what to watch
1. **AV unit economics, not headlines:** When robotaxi trips are material, is Uber's *per-trip take on AV rides* (demand-aggregation fee) accretive or dilutive vs. human-driver trips? Watch the Lucid–Nuro SF launch (late 2026) for the first real margin read.
2. **FCF conversion durability:** Can it hold >90% of EBITDA as working-capital tailwinds and SBC normalize? SBC-adjusted FCF is the honest number.
3. **Take-rate trajectory** by segment — is Delivery take expanding (ads/grocery mix) or has it plateaued?
4. **Buyback cadence:** Will the $20B authorization sustain ~$3B/qtr, or was Q1 a pull-forward? Net share-count reduction is the EPS lever.
5. **Gig-regulation calendar:** EU Platform Work Directive transposition and any US state reclassification rulings.

## 7. Bottom line
Uber is a genuinely de-risked *financial* story — accelerating 20–25% bookings, expanding margins, ~$10B FCF, aggressive buybacks, and a high-margin ads/membership layer — trading near a 52-week low at ~14–15x FCF / ~20x EV/EBITDA because the market is pricing AV as a threat rather than (potentially) a distribution opportunity Uber is hedging with $600M+ of AV bets. **Base case 12–24mo total return ~+30–45%** (toward ~$100), with bull ~+90% and a real bear to ~−25% if AV disintermediates the marketplace or gig-reclassification lands. **A reasonable 3–4% initiating position** for a PM who wants a diversifying, cheap-PEG platform compounder and can stomach the binary AV tail.
