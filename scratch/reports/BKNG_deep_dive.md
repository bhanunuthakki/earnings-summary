# Booking Holdings (BKNG) — Bull / Bear / Valuation Deep-Dive
*As of 2026-06-07 · for a sophisticated PM evaluating a new position*

## 1. Snapshot
- **Price ~$165** (prev close $167.49); **market cap ~$120–128B**; FY26e P/E ~16x, TTM P/E 19.9x, P/FCF 13.3x. **Note: BKNG executed a 25-for-1 split effective April 6, 2026** — the ~$165 quote equals ~$4,125 pre-split, so all historical charts/EPS must be ÷25 to compare.
- **Business:** the world's largest OTA — Booking.com (accommodations engine, ~90% of profit), Priceline, Agoda (Asia), Kayak (metasearch), OpenTable (restaurants). 2025: $186B gross bookings, $26.9B revenue, $9.1B FCF. CEO Glenn Fogel.
- **Central debate:** a wide-moat, ~34%-EBITDA-margin FCF machine retiring ~4–5%/yr of shares *on sale* after a FY26 guidance cut — **vs.** travel cyclicality, a real geopolitical demand shock, and the genuine risk that AI/agentic search collapses the lucrative search→book funnel.

## 2. Bull case (what must go right)
1. **Buyback math is the core engine.** Diluted shares fell from 1,200M (2018) → 816M (2025), ~32% in seven years. Q1'26 returned a **record $4.0B** ($3.6B buyback + $0.3B dividend), shrinking share count **−4% YoY**. With ~$24B remaining on the authorization and ~$9B annual FCF, BKNG can retire ~4–6%/yr. That converts ~8% revenue growth into the **~17% forward EPS CAGR** (FY26e $10.46 → FY28e $14.35) priced by consensus.
2. **Margin expansion is delivering.** Operating margin went 27.3% (2023) → 31.8% (2024) → **34.5% (2025)**; the Transformation Program targets **$550M run-rate savings by end-2026**. Adjusted EBITDA margin ~37%.
3. **Connected Trip + alternative accommodations.** Alt-accom listings hit a record **8.6M (+8%)**; travelers booking multiple trip components **+20%**; flights/payments attach lifts engagement and (via merchant model) take rate.
4. **Genius loyalty flywheel:** **50% of bookings** from Genius members (only 30% of active travelers) — higher direct-mix, lower marketing dependence, a partial hedge against Google.
5. **Capital-return optionality:** dividend initiated 2024, since raised ~9% (TTM $1.57/share, ~1.0% yield), payout only ~21% of earnings — room to grow alongside buybacks.

## 3. Bear case (genuine skeptic)
1. **AI/agentic disintermediation — the real long-term threat.** Bernstein calls it a "paradigm shift" potentially bigger than Google's entry a decade ago. **Google AI Mode (75M+ DAUs)** is testing **direct hotel-booking links** that bypass OTA aggregators; if AI agents become the discovery layer, BKNG's customer-acquisition moat and take rate compress. *Mitigant:* in **March 2026 OpenAI pulled its ChatGPT "Buy Now" checkout** (travel "too complex") — BKNG popped +8% — and Booking/Expedia are positioning as the *fulfillment* layer agents call. Net: a slow-burn margin risk, not an imminent cliff, but it caps the terminal multiple.
2. **Geopolitical demand shock is live.** The **Middle-East/Strait-of-Hormuz conflict cut ~2pts off Q1'26 room nights**; management **lowered FY26 guidance** from low-double-digit to **high-single→low-single-digit** bookings/revenue growth, with Q2 room nights guided to just **2–4%**. Recovery is *assumed* in H2 — execution risk.
3. **Travel cyclicality / consumer-discretionary beta (1.10).** A US recession hits discretionary travel hard; 2020 showed revenue −55% and FCF negative. US already lagging Europe/Asia.
4. **Regulation:** EU DMA designates Booking.com a gatekeeper (no wide parity clauses, self-preferencing limits); ongoing antitrust/consumer scrutiny in the EU.
5. **Competition & FX:** ABNB in alt-accom, EXPE re-energized (Uber distribution deal, cheaper); ~85% of bookings are non-USD, so a stronger dollar is a headwind (FX is a ~+2pt *tailwind* in the current FY26 guide, which can reverse).

## 4. Valuation
- **vs. history:** Forward P/E ~16x sits **below BKNG's own ~22–24x 5-yr average** — the post-guidance-cut selloff (~−16% off the $233 high; 52-wk range ~$151–$234) re-rated it to the low end.
- **vs. peers (early-April):** BKNG fwd P/E ~15.6x vs **EXPE ~11.8x** and **ABNB ~25.4x**; EV/EBITDA ~13.3x vs EXPE 12.5x, ABNB 25.8x. BKNG screens reasonable for the highest-quality, highest-margin name.
- **Cached DCFs (~$259) — haircut, don't parrot.** FMP's perpetuity model implies ~57% upside; it over-credits a high-FCF compounder. **Naive cross-check:** FCF ~$9.1B / ~$124B cap = **~7.3% FCF yield**; at ~7–8% FCF growth + ~4–5%/yr share shrink, a *flat multiple* yields a **~12–15% IRR**.
- **Reverse-DCF / what's priced in:** at ~16x FY26e, the market is discounting the guide-down, implying high-single-digit bookings growth and steady-to-slightly-lower margins — i.e., **low expectations**, the bull's entry argument.

## 5. Portfolio fit
- **Diversification is strong.** The book is concentrated in LatAm (MELI+NU ≈24%) and enterprise software (≈28%). BKNG is a **developed-market (Europe/Asia-skewed) consumer-discretionary travel** name — a genuinely new vector. Your screen (ann vol 30%, **corr 0.53**, **marginal-Sharpe +1.06, 2nd best**) supports it; the moderate correlation reflects shared "growth/risk-on" beta, not business overlap.
- **MELI overlap — modest but real.** MELI has a consumer-discretionary + LatAm-consumer tilt. BKNG is barely present in LatAm and is asset-light/no-credit-risk, so the overlap is **thematic (discretionary consumer) not structural** — both would suffer in a global consumer downturn, the one correlated tail to size around.
- **Suggested initial size: 3–4%** (funded partly from SGOV/SPY). High single-name vol (30%) and a live geopolitical/AI overhang argue for a starter, not a full weight; the favorable marginal-Sharpe and cheap entry justify initiating now with room to add.

## 6. Key diligence questions & what to watch
1. **AI funnel:** Track Google AI Mode direct-booking rollout and BKNG's performance-marketing efficiency (marketing % of bookings) — the leading indicator of disintermediation.
2. **FY26 guide trajectory:** Does Q2 land in the 2–4% room-night range and does management reaffirm an **H2 recovery**? A second cut would break the "trough" thesis.
3. **Take rate / merchant mix:** Is revenue/bookings still expanding as payments and flights scale?
4. **Buyback pace:** Confirm continued ~$3.5–4B/quarter; a pause would gut the EPS-growth bridge.
5. **EU DMA / regulatory rulings** affecting parity and self-preferencing.

## 7. Bottom line
BKNG is a best-in-class, cash-gushing OTA compounder trading **~16x forward / ~7% FCF yield — below its own history and a fraction of ABNB** — because a real (but quantified, ~2pt) geopolitical shock forced a FY26 guide-down. The buyback does the heavy lifting: ~8% bookings growth becomes ~17% EPS growth. The legitimate long-term worry is AI/agentic disintermediation of the search→book funnel, which I'd treat as a multiple-cap, not a 2026 earnings event (reinforced by OpenAI's March checkout retreat). **Initiate a 3–4% starter; base-case 12–24mo total return ~+25% to +35%** (target ~$210–225 + ~1% yield), with add-points on H2 demand confirmation.
