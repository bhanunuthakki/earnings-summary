# Living interaction contract

**Status:** canonical interaction boundary. This concise contract retains the active laws from
`interaction_paradigm_2026_06.md`; that program remains historical evidence only.

## Current laws

- **Identity before source:** user-visible labels, rank, actions, and links derive from stored
  semantic identity through the shared resolver, never source-table names, raw enums, or render-time
  string heuristics.
- **Depth is a doorway:** a datum with a deeper view is an accessible link or button with one
  shell-handled action. Tooltips supplement rather than replace a doorway; stable fact references
  are handles, not display labels.
- **Transient surfaces share the overlay contract:** use the registered overlay/open-surface stack
  with appropriate close, Escape, scrim, and focus behavior. A panel under labeled navigation does
  not reprint its navigation title or stack more than one operating band.
- **One content boundary per kind:** stored prose uses the canonical prose renderer; existing
  shared renderers for toolbars, provenance, ticker labels, and other registered primitives remain
  the executable owner.
- **Truthful interaction:** controls disclose their read-only or mutating effect and route to their
  typed behavior/state owner. Comments and Copilot behavior live in
  `directives/report_comments_and_chat.md`; provenance meaning in `directives/data_provenance.md`;
  and operator controls in `directives/operations_governance_surface.md`.

The project design registry, shell tests, and UI controls are executable detail authorities.
Navigation proposals remain non-governing until owner approval.
