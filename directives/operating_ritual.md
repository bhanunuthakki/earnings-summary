# Explicit research and decision logging

The October 25 owner cleanup supersedes the former coaching ritual. Basic
logging and decision time series remain. Automatic nudges, personal-review
packets, proactive standups and Telegram capture are retired in the local
candidate. Canonical deployment and stored-data cleanup remain separate steps.

Record an outcome when it is useful. Use the Notes capture box or a project agent.
Use `execution/land_session_notes.py --db <configured-db>` for an explicit note,
intent resolution or typed decision checkpoint. See `claude_session_bridge.md`.
Do not retain whole chats as a replacement input library.

Keep a dated decision, its stated reason, size, conviction, falsifier and later
outcome when the owner supplies them. Missing values remain missing. Use existing
confirmation and correction writers. Do not infer an owner commitment from a
model recommendation.

Ask targeted company, thesis, valuation or portfolio questions through Copilot or
a project agent using `src/advisor/skills/earnings-summary-investing/SKILL.md`.
Use existing verified facts and source manifests. A research request does not
silently authorize new collection or generation beyond its approved scope.

Read operational failures in Operations. Its Jobs view owns the existing seven-day
history and diagnostic alarms. A healthy process does not prove source coverage
or decision-grade financial evidence.
